Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the Kansō Sudoku iPhone app and its support pages handle information.

Game progress

Puzzles, notes, settings and game history are stored on your device to save your progress and show your statistics. Deleting the app removes its local game data from that device.

App Store purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat assigns a generated app user ID and processes purchase and entitlement information to verify purchases, unlock Premium and restore access. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Advertising

When ads are enabled, the free edition uses Google AdMob to request non-personalized ads. Google may process device and network information, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver, measure and protect its ads. When available, manage your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Privacy controls

To remove local game data, delete the app from your iPhone. For data held by Apple or RevenueCat, see the privacy resources linked under App Store purchases.
