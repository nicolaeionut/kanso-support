Support

Help with Kansō Sudoku on iPhone, from resuming a puzzle to restoring Premium.

Restore Premium

Open Kansō Sudoku, select Premium and tap Restore Purchases. Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account you used to purchase Premium.

What Premium includes

Kansō Premium is a one-time in-app purchase. It unlocks three exclusive themes, unlimited hints, the daily challenge archive, and ad-free play.

Billing and refunds

Apple handles App Store payments. View your purchase history or request a refund from Apple.

Apple purchase support

Saved puzzles

Your puzzles, preferences and game history are stored on your device. Open the app to resume your last game.
